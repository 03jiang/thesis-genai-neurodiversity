# Selected aggregated outputs

This directory contains selected non-sensitive, aggregated figures and tables corresponding to the final submitted thesis. Participant-level tables and outputs that could reveal individual responses are excluded.

Included outputs are the K-Means and K-Modes diagnostics and the aggregate Question 5 comparison. The PCA projection is excluded even though it appears in the thesis because every plotted point represents an individual response and its marker encodes the voluntary self-identification answer. Obsolete Q5 validation and PCA loadings outputs are also excluded.
